One line of earned pride, for the make.fun "One Line of Earned Pride" bounty. A father runs behind his daughter's bicycle for three blocks, then lets go at the corner without saying a word — and she rides the rest of the street alone, ringing the bell twice. No trophy, no speech, just a small person doing something hard while nobody is watching. The line:

> He ran behind her bicycle for three blocks and let go at the corner without telling her — and she rode the rest of the street alone, ringing the bell twice.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Running it validates the line (non-empty, a single line, at most 240 characters) and writes the entry to /output/entry.txt.
